# Prosecution rebuttal

## Conceded
The defense genuinely wins **responsible design (4)**. Keeping the model off the knobs, requiring step-up approval, and logging every action maps cleanly onto Microsoft's July 2026 least-privilege guidance, and the honest "can't do 90%" answer is a real strength. I no longer contest that criterion. I also concede the scale objection is blunted by the builder's candor: a judge will not mark them down for hiding the toy size.

## Where the defense does not hold

**"The claim is the control layer, not the wattage."** This is the defense's best move, but it shifts the target. If the claim is the control layer, then "measurable usefulness" is measured against a baseline the builder authored on three jobs the builder chose. The defense never answers that charge. Watts on a meter prove the loop closes, not that it beats what operators already do.

**"Seams are production path."** A pluggable channel, an LLM abstraction and a documented Teams adapter are interfaces, not integrations. The defense itself calls Teams "documented, not built" and offers an "FDE will value the what-changes-at-100-MW list" point, which it labels speculation. A list is a slide. Criterion 3 asks for a path, and the defense offers intentions.

**"Sim mode makes failure graceful."** It cuts the other way. If the meter, network or box dies, the judge is watching a simulation in a challenge titled "Built for the Real World," and the end-to-end claim (5) quietly becomes "end-to-end in a simulator." The defense concedes the live-demo risk in its last line and never prices it.

**"Lamp effect is verifiable by eye at 1 Hz."** A lamp adds tens of watts, and the compensating GPU clock change is a few watts under smoothing. The defense asserts visibility but offers no reason the judge can separate cause from noise inside a 4-minute slot.

**"Emerald validates the market."** True for the problem, irrelevant for the win. The defense's differentiator, that incumbents will not expose approval and verification internals, is again flagged speculation.

**"FDEs are the nearest customer."** Judges are not operators. Customer understanding (1) still rests on dockets and press.

## Unanswered
The defense never answers charge 7: priorities, deadlines and permissions are structured inputs, so a solver produces the same plan. Until someone shows what the LLM contributes that a rules engine could not, the "agent" is a narrator.

## Net
Responsible design is the defense's. Production path (3) and usefulness (2) remain unrebutted in substance.
